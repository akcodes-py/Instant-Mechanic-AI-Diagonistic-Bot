# H3 spatial conventions

| Resolution | Approx. edge | Used for |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **8** | ~460 m | Live mechanic presence (Cassandra/Redis), `gridDisk` ring expansion (k=1/2/3), offer matching |
| **7** | ~1.22 km | Kafka partition key (localizes city-zone traffic), React supply/demand heatmap, PySpark aggregation |

## Ring expansion

`h3.gridDisk(origin_res8, k)`:

| k | Hexes | Typical use |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | 7 | nearest supply (~460 m–1 km) |
| 2 | 19 | first cascade expansion |
| 3 | 37 | last expansion before hotline escalation |

`cascade_rings()` yields *newly added* cells per hop (7 / 12 / 18), so the
cumulative coverage after each hop is 7 / 19 / 37 and no mechanic is offered twice.

Cascade order: for each ring, filter ONLINE mechanics with the required skill,
rank by ML ETA (fallback: nearest distance), acquire the 15 s Redis lock, push the
exclusive offer. On decline/timeout, release and continue; after k=3, emit
`ESCALATED` and surface the emergency hotline.

## Coordinate conventions

- WGS84 decimal degrees; `lat` then `lon` everywhere in events and UI.
- `latLngToCell(lat, lon, res)` for indexing; `cellToLatLng(cell)` for map overlays.
- H3 cell IDs are lowercase hex strings (15 characters). The **second** hex character is the resolution nibble (e.g. `883da11a17fffff` is Res-8, its parent `873da11a1ffffff` is Res-7). Validate with `h3.get_resolution()`, not by string prefix.
- Python: `h3` 4.x. Frontend: `h3-js` 4.x. Kotlin: `com.uber:h3` 4.x. All must agree on the cell string; no integer conversion across languages.

## Delhi NCR demo bounds (Gurugram pilot zone)

The H3 design covers the whole Delhi NCR region. Default simulator area: centre `28.4595, 77.0266` (Sector 75, Gurugram), radius ~5 km — one sample NCR zone, not the coverage limit.
No real mechanic locations or customer PII are committed; the simulator generates
synthetic mechanics.
